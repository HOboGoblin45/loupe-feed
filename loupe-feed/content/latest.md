- **algorithm** — Same reason. Nothing on this sheet was decided by one.
- **recommend / recommended for you** — We are reporting what a shop did, not telling anyone what to buy.
- **personalised / personalized** — None of these numbers are about the viewer.
- **stunning, must-have, obsessed, iconic, insane** — Hype is what everyone else has. A number is what we have.
